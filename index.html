The Essence of Our Craft

About Us

Zivarno, derived from the amalgamation of the Persian word Zindagi meaning life and the Sanskrit word Varna meaning colors, embodies the very essence of our brand.

We celebrate the vibrant tapestry of life through exquisite craftsmanship and exceptional service, setting ourselves apart from our peers in the art and handicraft industry. Our creations reflect the richness of life’s hues, bringing artistry and elegance into every piece.

Based in historic Calicut (Kozhikode), Kerala—the legendary port of ancient maritime spices and artisan craft—Zivarno connects master generational artisans with discerning patrons across the globe.

Our Ethical Mission

Our Vision

Zivarno is dedicated to uplifting local artisans by bridging cultures and fostering sustainable livelihoods, enhancing global appreciation for their exquisite, handmade creations. We provide a vital platform that reconnects talented artisans with an international audience, reviving traditional craftsmanship and celebrating the rich heritage of Asia through timeless treasures. Each piece tells a story, inviting you to partake in the beauty of artistry that transcends borders.
